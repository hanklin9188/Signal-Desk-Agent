# Research Evidence Skill

This skill is optional and not part of the SignalDesk MVP path.

It may reuse the existing Research Evidence Agent for:

- searching local papers;
- extracting metrics;
- calculations;
- notes.

It must remain isolated from private message connectors and must not cause Gmail/LINE/Messenger content to enter the research document index.
